# Project Lighthouse
### Global Knowledge Infrastructure Program

*A simulated 18-month digital transformation and delivery program for a large, globally operating consulting firm.*

---

## Executive Summary

Project Lighthouse introduces a governed, searchable, AI-enabled Knowledge Infrastructure platform that lets consultants at a global professional services firm find and reuse organizational knowledge — case work, methodologies, expertise, and client credentials — far faster than today's fragmented tools (SharePoint sites, shared drives, email) allow. The program is documented as one connected business case across two repositories: one demonstrating **digital transformation leadership** (strategy, operating model, change), and one demonstrating **project management delivery** (planning, execution, control) of the same initiative.

---

## Background

**Solstice Advisory** is a fictional global consulting firm operating across multiple practices, industries, and geographies, with thousands of consultants generating case work, frameworks, and expertise every day. Over time, this knowledge has become scattered across disconnected systems, inconsistently classified, and difficult to search — particularly across regional and practice boundaries. Meanwhile, high analyst and associate turnover means tacit knowledge is regularly lost, and proposals are often rebuilt from scratch rather than reusing prior credentials and case assets.

At the same time, Solstice's knowledge carries constraints that a generic enterprise doesn't face: client-confidentiality walls between competing engagements, contractual restrictions on reusing client deliverables, and cross-border data regulations across the regions it operates in. Any solution has to work *with* these constraints, not around them.

**The problem, in short:** valuable knowledge exists across the firm, but consultants can't reliably find it, trust it, or reuse it — and the cost of that friction compounds daily, globally, at scale.

---

## The Proposal

A **Knowledge Infrastructure platform** — governed taxonomy, unified search, and an AI-assisted retrieval layer — built on a target operating model that fits how a global consulting firm actually works: federated governance, practice/industry/geography-aware classification, and confidentiality-aware access by design.

**Indicative Value**

An estimated 15 minutes/day per consultant currently lost to searching for information, at scale, represents a significant recurring cost. A 20% improvement in search efficiency translates to an estimated **€1.2M/year** in recovered productivity — against a €750K investment — alongside faster proposal turnaround, higher reuse of firm IP, and reduced knowledge loss from attrition.

## Why Two Repositories

A real transformation of this kind requires operating at two altitudes at once: shaping the strategy and organizational change, and disciplined execution of the underlying project. Rather than blending both into one document set, Project Lighthouse separates them so each can be evaluated on its own terms — while remaining explicitly linked as one continuous narrative and business case.
