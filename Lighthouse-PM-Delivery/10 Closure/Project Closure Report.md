# Project Closure Report
### Knowledge Infrastructure Workstream — Project Lighthouse

**Status:** Closed
**Closure Date:** Month 18
**Related:** `Benefits Realization.xlsx`, `Lessons Learned.md`, `Retrospective.md`

---

## 1. Summary

Project Lighthouse's Knowledge Infrastructure workstream delivered a governed, searchable, AI-enabled knowledge platform to Solstice Advisory's global consultant base across EMEA, Americas, and APAC, completing within the approved 18-month timeline and within budget (inclusive of one approved contingency draw).

## 2. Delivery Against Charter Objectives

| Objective | Result |
|---|---|
| Consolidate fragmented knowledge sources | 94% of content migrated and classified (target: 90%) |
| Improve search effectiveness | Search success rate reached 86% (target: 85%) |
| Enable AI-assisted retrieval | AI-answer grounding accuracy reached 96% (target: 95%) |
| Establish sustainable governance | 94% of active documents have an assigned owner (target: 95% — near miss) |
| Drive adoption | 74% active usage within 3 months of launch (target: 70%) |

## 3. Budget Outcome

Final spend: approximately €745,000 against a €750,000 approved budget, including a €15,000 contingency draw approved under Decision 001. €15,000 of the original €30,000 contingency reserve remained unused at closure.

## 4. Schedule Outcome

Delivered on the original 18-month end date. One schedule adjustment was made during delivery (Decision 003 — phased AI assistant release in EMEA), absorbed within the existing rollout buffer without affecting the overall program end date.

## 5. Key Decisions During Delivery

- **Decision 001** — Budget change due to migration complexity (Month 5)
- **Decision 002** — Taxonomy governance approach adopted (Month 3)
- **Decision 003** — Launch date adjustment for the AI assistant feature (Month 13)

## 6. Outstanding Items at Closure

- Content review completion (88% vs. 90% target) — transitioned to business-as-usual governance via the Stale Content Alerts automation
- APAC active usage still ramping at closure; regional Knowledge Contact to continue monitoring post-transition
