# Communication Plan
### Knowledge Infrastructure Workstream — Project Lighthouse

**Version:** 1.0
**Related Documents:** `Stakeholder Register.xlsx`, `06 Communications/`

---

## 1. Purpose

This plan defines what is communicated, to whom, how, and how often, across the life of the project. Communication is differentiated by audience because executives, the project team, employees, and knowledge owners each need different information to do their part.

## 2. Communication Matrix

| Audience | Purpose | Content Focus | Format | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Executive Sponsor & Steering Committee | Decision-making, escalation | Overall health, budget, schedule, risks, decisions required, business benefits | Steering committee deck, executive status report | Monthly | Program Manager |
| Regional Managing Partners | Regional buy-in and readiness | Regional migration timing, impact, support needed | Executive briefing | Per migration wave | Program Manager |
| Project Team (Knowledge Governance Lead, Regional Contacts, Technology Lead) | Coordination and execution | Tasks, dependencies, issues, upcoming work, decisions, actions | Weekly update, working sessions | Weekly | Project Manager |
| Practice Area Leaders | Input and validation | Taxonomy design, practice-specific classification needs | Briefing email, working session | As needed during design phases | Knowledge Governance Lead |
| Consultants (End Users) | Awareness and adoption | What is changing, why, what to do, when, where to get help | Announcement email, newsletter, FAQ, in-platform guidance | At key milestones (kickoff, pilot, go-live) | Change Lead |
| Compliance & Data Privacy Office | Assurance and sign-off | Ethical-wall design, data residency approach | Governance review meeting | Per governance milestone | Knowledge Governance Lead |
| HR / People Team | Alignment on incentives | Incentive design, offboarding knowledge capture process | Briefing email | As needed | Change Lead |

## 3. Escalation Path

1. Issue identified by project team → logged in `05 Controls/Change Request Log.xlsx` or `02 Planning/RAID Log.xlsx`
2. Assessed by Project Manager for impact (scope, budget, schedule, risk)
3. If within delegated authority, resolved directly and communicated to affected stakeholders
4. If exceeding delegated authority, escalated to Program Steering Committee via escalation email or steering deck
5. Decision recorded in `09 Decisions/`

## 4. Standard Templates

See `06 Communications/Templates/` for reusable formats:
- Status Report
- Decision Request
- Escalation Email
- Meeting Agenda

## 5. Feedback Channels

- Weekly project team retrospective for internal feedback
- In-platform feedback widget during pilot and rollout for end-user input
- Regional Knowledge Contacts as a standing feedback conduit from local offices
