# Sprint Report — Sprint 0 (Discovery)
### Knowledge Search & AI Assistant Workstream

**Duration:** Month 6, Weeks 1–2
**Goal:** Define MVP scope and success metrics for the search and AI assistant capability

---

## Summary

Conducted structured interviews with 18 consultants across three practices (Strategy, Digital, Operations) and two regions (EMEA, Americas) to understand current search pain points. Completed technical discovery on the candidate search platform and vector database options.

## Key Findings

- Consultants reported spending an average of 15+ minutes per search session, often re-searching for content they recalled seeing before but could not relocate
- Keyword search alone was cited as the top limitation of existing tools — consultants often know *what they mean* but not the *exact terms* used in the original document
- Regional offices expressed concern about content from other regions/practices cluttering search results — reinforcing the need for taxonomy-based filtering from the outset

## MVP Definition

1. Keyword search across migrated content (Sprint 1)
2. Metadata filtering by taxonomy spine (Sprint 2)
3. Semantic search (Sprint 3)
4. Pilot with a real user group before AI assistant investment (Sprint 4)

## Success Metrics Established

| Metric | Baseline | Target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Search success rate | 54% | 85% |
| AI-answer grounding accuracy | — | 95% |
