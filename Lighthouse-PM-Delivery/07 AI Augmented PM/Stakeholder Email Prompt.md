# AI-Augmented PM: Stakeholder Communication Drafting

## Use Case
Drafting the employee-facing go-live announcement for EMEA, tailored to a general consultant audience rather than a technical or executive one.

## Prompt Used

```
Draft a short, warm, plain-language email to consultants announcing that
our new Knowledge Platform is going live in EMEA this week. Key facts:

- Replaces fragmented SharePoint/shared drive search with one unified
  platform
- Search and filtering are live now; the AI assistant feature is delayed
  because pilot testing showed it wasn't accurate enough yet
- No new login needed — uses existing credentials
- Old SharePoint stays available read-only for 30 days as a fallback
- Support available via an in-platform channel and regional Knowledge
  Contacts

Tone: honest and matter-of-fact about the AI delay rather than spinning
it as a non-issue — consultants are sophisticated and will see through
a forced positive spin. Keep it under 200 words.
```

## Example Output (AI Draft)

> Hi all, starting this week, our new Knowledge Platform is live for EMEA... [see full version in `06 Communications/Employees/Announcement Email - EMEA Go-Live.md`]

## What the PM Did With It

Kept the AI-drafted honesty about the AI assistant delay (rather than softening it, which was the PM's instinct on a first read) since early feedback from the Sprint 4 pilot suggested consultants respond better to direct communication about known limitations. Added the specific links and support channel details at the end.
