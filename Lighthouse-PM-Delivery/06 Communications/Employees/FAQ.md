# Knowledge Platform — FAQ
### EMEA Launch

---

**Q: Do I need a new login?**
No — the platform uses your existing firm credentials.

**Q: Where did the AI assistant go? I heard about it.**
It's coming soon, not included in this initial launch. Early testing showed it wasn't yet accurate enough to trust for client-facing work, so we're holding it back until it meets our quality bar. Standard search is available now and covers all migrated content.

**Q: Will I still be able to access old SharePoint sites?**
Yes, for 30 days after go-live, in read-only mode, as a fallback while everyone transitions. After that, migrated content will only live on the new platform.

**Q: What if I can't find something that used to be on SharePoint?**
Check the FAQ's "Report Missing Content" link, or contact your regional Knowledge Contact — some very old or low-quality content was archived rather than migrated; we can help you locate it if needed.

**Q: Can people from other regions see my client's confidential work?**
No. The platform enforces the same client-confidentiality and ethical-wall protections we already follow — if anything, access controls are more consistent now than across the old scattered systems.

**Q: What should I do if I think something is misclassified or missing an owner?**
Flag it directly in the platform — this feeds into our ongoing content quality review.

**Q: Who do I contact for help?**
Your regional Knowledge Contact, or the in-platform support channel.
