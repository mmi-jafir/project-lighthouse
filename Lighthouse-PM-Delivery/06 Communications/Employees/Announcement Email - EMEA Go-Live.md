# Introducing the New Knowledge Platform — Live in EMEA This Week

**To:** All EMEA Consultants

---

Hi all,

Starting this week, our new Knowledge Platform is live for EMEA. This replaces the fragmented search across SharePoint, shared drives, and email with one place to find case work, methodologies, and expertise across the firm.

**What's changing:**
Search now covers all migrated EMEA content, with filters by practice, industry, and document type — no more guessing which SharePoint site something lives on.

**What's not changing yet:**
The AI assistant (ask-a-question style search) is still being refined and will follow in the coming weeks — we'd rather launch it once it's genuinely reliable than rush it out. Standard search is ready now and already a significant improvement.

**What you need to do:**
Log in with your existing credentials — no new account needed. A short walkthrough video and FAQ are linked below.

**Where to get help:**
Reach out via the in-platform support channel or your regional Knowledge Contact.

Thanks for your patience as we roll this out — feedback is genuinely welcome and will shape what comes next.
