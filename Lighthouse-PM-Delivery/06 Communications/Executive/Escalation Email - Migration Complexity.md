# Escalation

**To:** Executive Sponsor, Program Steering Committee
**From:** Program Manager
**Date:** Month 5
**Severity:** High

---

## Issue
The Wave 1 (EMEA) migration audit has identified that legacy content metadata quality is significantly below the original estimate, increasing the manual classification effort required for the remaining migration waves (Americas, APAC) by an estimated 30%.

## Why This Requires Escalation
The projected additional effort exceeds what can be absorbed within the current Migration budget line (€120,000) without a budget, scope, or schedule adjustment — all of which require Steering Committee approval per the project's change control threshold (€10,000+).

## Impact if Unresolved
Without a decision, Wave 2 (Americas) migration, scheduled to begin Month 8, will proceed at reduced quality (incomplete classification) or stall pending funding clarity — either of which risks the program's global rollout timeline.

## Recommended Action
Approve a partial contingency draw (€15,000) to fund an automated metadata-cleanup utility for Waves 2–3, retaining €15,000 in reserve. Full analysis and options are detailed in the accompanying Decision Request.

## Timeline
Response requested before the Month 5 Steering Committee meeting to avoid delaying Wave 2 preparation.
