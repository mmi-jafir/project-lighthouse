# Project Charter
### Knowledge Infrastructure Workstream — Project Lighthouse

**Firm:** Solstice Advisory (fictional, global consulting firm)
**Program:** Project Lighthouse — Global Knowledge Infrastructure Program
**Document Owner:** Program Manager
**Status:** Approved
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 1. Project Purpose

Solstice Advisory's organizational knowledge — case work, methodologies, expertise, and client credentials — is fragmented across SharePoint sites, shared drives, and email, and is inconsistently governed across practices, industries, and geographies. Consultants lose significant time daily searching for information that likely already exists, proposals are frequently rebuilt from scratch, and expertise is difficult to locate across a global, matrixed firm.

This project will deliver a governed, searchable, AI-enabled **Knowledge Infrastructure platform** that lets consultants find and reuse organizational knowledge faster and more reliably than today's tools allow, while respecting the firm's confidentiality and conflict-of-interest constraints.

## 2. Business Case Summary

An estimated 15 minutes/day lost per consultant to searching for information, across the firm's global consultant base, represents a significant recurring productivity cost. A 20% improvement in search efficiency is estimated to translate to approximately **€1.2M/year** in recovered productivity, against a **€750,000** investment — alongside faster proposal turnaround and reduced knowledge loss from staff attrition.

*(All figures are simulated/estimated for portfolio purposes.)*

## 3. Objectives

| # | Objective | Success Measure |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Consolidate fragmented knowledge sources into a governed platform | 90%+ of prioritized content migrated and classified |
| 2 | Improve search effectiveness | Search success rate improves from 54% to 85% |
| 3 | Enable AI-assisted retrieval | AI-answer grounding accuracy ≥ 95% |
| 4 | Establish sustainable governance | Ownership assigned to 95%+ of active documents |
| 5 | Drive adoption across the firm | 70%+ active usage among target consultant population within 3 months of launch |

## 4. Scope Summary

**In scope:** knowledge audit and migration, information architecture and taxonomy, platform implementation, AI-assisted search (RAG layer), governance model and processes, change management and training, automation of platform operations.

**Out of scope:** redesign of the firm's broader IT infrastructure unrelated to knowledge management; replacement of client-facing delivery tools; compensation or staffing model changes (addressed only at a policy-recommendation level under change management).

*(See `Scope Statement.md` for full detail.)*

## 7. Governance & Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Executive Sponsor | Overall accountability, funding authority, escalation resolution |
| Program Steering Committee | Strategic oversight, gate approvals, major risk decisions |
| Project Manager | Day-to-day planning, execution, control, and reporting |
| Knowledge Governance Lead | Taxonomy, classification, and content lifecycle ownership |
| Regional Leads | Local adoption, migration coordination, stakeholder liaison |
| Technology Lead | Platform, search, and AI/RAG implementation oversight |

## 8. Key Assumptions

- Executive sponsorship and budget remain stable throughout the 18-month duration.
- Regional offices provide a designated Knowledge Governance contact.
- Existing content sources (SharePoint, Confluence, shared drives) remain accessible for audit and migration throughout the program.

## 9. Key Constraints

- Client-confidentiality and ethical-wall requirements must be respected in access design.
- Cross-border data residency regulations apply across regions of operation.
- Migration cannot disrupt active client engagements.
