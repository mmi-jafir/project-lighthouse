# Incentive and Adoption Strategy
### Project Lighthouse — Knowledge Infrastructure Transformation

**Version:** 1.0
**Related:** `Capability And Culture Plan.md`, `Lighthouse-PM-Delivery/02 Planning/RAID Log.xlsx` (R003)

---

## 1. The Core Problem

Solstice Advisory's consultants are incentivized primarily through billable-hour targets and client delivery performance. Contributing to knowledge capture — writing a case debrief, tagging content correctly, flagging outdated material — is non-billable time with no direct career or compensation benefit. Training and communication alone will not overcome this structural disincentive.

## 2. Strategic Approach

Rather than relying solely on awareness campaigns, this strategy addresses the incentive structure directly, in partnership with HR:

| Lever | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Staffing credit | Time spent on case debriefs and content stewardship counts toward utilization targets, within a defined monthly cap |
| Recognition | Knowledge contribution is a named factor in practice-level recognition programs (not performance review scoring, to avoid gaming) |
| Reduced friction | AI-assisted drafting (see PM Delivery repository's AI-Augmented PM practice) lowers the effort required to produce a quality debrief, making the ask smaller |
| Visible reciprocity | Consultants see, in-platform, when their contributed content is reused by others — making the benefit of contribution tangible rather than abstract |

## 3. What This Strategy Deliberately Avoids

- Making knowledge contribution a formal performance review criterion, which risks incentivizing volume over quality and creating a compliance-driven rather than genuine knowledge culture
- Mandating a fixed quota of contributions per consultant, which similarly risks low-effort compliance behavior

## 4. Adoption Approach by Grade

| Grade | Adoption Focus |
|---|---|
| Partners / Principals | Model behavior by requesting and citing platform content directly in client discussions |
| Managers / Consultants | Primary users of search and AI assistant; case debrief authors |
| Analysts / Associates | Heaviest search users; least tenure to contribute institutional debriefs, so onboarding-stage knowledge capture (see Capability and Culture Plan) is prioritized for this group |

## 5. Measurement

Adoption is tracked via active usage rate and debrief completion rate (see `../06 Value Realization/Transformation KPIs.md`), reviewed alongside — not in isolation from — utilization data, to catch any early sign that the staffing-credit mechanism is being gamed rather than genuinely used.
