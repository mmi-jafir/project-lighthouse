# Change Fatigue and Sequencing
### Project Lighthouse — Knowledge Infrastructure Transformation

**Version:** 1.0
**Related:** `Incentive And Adoption Strategy.md`, `../03 Portfolio And Roadmap/Transformation Roadmap.md`

---

## 1. The Risk

Solstice Advisory's consultants already absorb frequent change — new engagement tools, evolving methodology, shifting client demands. A knowledge platform rollout competing for attention alongside other firm initiatives risks being deprioritized or experienced as "yet another mandatory tool," regardless of its actual value.

## 2. Sequencing Principles to Manage Fatigue

1. **Stagger, don't stack** — regional go-lives are sequenced (EMEA → Americas → APAC) rather than simultaneous, so no single period asks the whole firm to absorb change at once
2. **Decouple stable from experimental** — as demonstrated at the delivery level (see `Lighthouse-PM-Delivery/09 Decisions/003 Launch Date Adjustment.md`), launching a reliable core experience separately from a still-maturing AI feature avoids asking consultants to tolerate rough edges in everything at once
3. **Minimize simultaneous asks** — training, communications, and incentive changes (e.g., the staffing-credit mechanism) are introduced on a light cadence around each regional go-live rather than all at program kickoff
4. **Protect billable capacity during go-live weeks** — regional rollout weeks avoid scheduling additional mandatory firm-wide training on unrelated topics, coordinated with HR's broader training calendar

## 3. Signals of Change Fatigue to Monitor

- Declining engagement with in-platform feedback requests over time
- Passive compliance (logging in once, then reverting to old habits) rather than active use
- Negative sentiment specifically referencing "another new tool" in feedback channels

## 4. Response If Fatigue Signals Appear

If fatigue signals appear during a regional rollout, the response is to **slow the remaining rollout cadence**, not to add more communications volume — additional messaging compounds fatigue rather than resolving it.
