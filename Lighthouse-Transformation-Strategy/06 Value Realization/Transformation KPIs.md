# Transformation KPIs
### Project Lighthouse — Knowledge Infrastructure Transformation

**Version:** 1.0
**Related:** `Benefits Register.xlsx`, `Value Realization Cadence.md`

---

## 1. Purpose

Defines the program-level KPIs used to judge whether the transformation is succeeding as a whole — distinct from the delivery-level KPIs (search success rate, grounding accuracy) tracked operationally in the PM Delivery repository.

## 2. Transformation-Level KPIs

| KPI | Baseline | Target | Measured By |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Active platform usage (3 months post-launch) | — | 70% of target consultant population | Platform usage logs |
| Case debrief completion rate | ~15% (estimated, informal) | 60% within 12 months | Debrief submissions vs. completed engagements |
| Proposal turnaround time | Current average | 20% reduction | Proposal tool timestamps |
| Time to locate a subject-matter expert | Anecdotally 1-2 days | Under 1 hour | Expert-finder usage / survey |
| Offboarding knowledge capture completion | ~0% (no formal process) | 90% of departing consultants | Offboarding checklist completion |
| Consultant-reported trust in platform content | — | 80%+ "would recommend to a colleague" | Periodic survey |

## 3. Distinction from Delivery KPIs

| Level | Example KPI | Owned By |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery (technical/operational) | Search success rate, AI grounding accuracy | Technology Lead — tracked in PM Delivery repository |
| Transformation (organizational/behavioral) | Debrief completion rate, expert-finder usage, cultural trust | Executive Sponsor / Knowledge Governance Council — tracked here |

Delivery KPIs answer "does the platform work?" Transformation KPIs answer "did the firm actually change how it works?" — both are necessary, but only the second confirms the transformation succeeded, not just the project.

## 4. Reporting

These KPIs are reported through the Quarterly Benefits Review and Annual Value Audit defined in `Value Realization Cadence.md`, not through the delivery project's monthly status reporting, to keep the distinction between "project delivered" and "transformation realized" clear even after program closure.
