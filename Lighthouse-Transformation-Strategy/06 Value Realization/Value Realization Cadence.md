# Value Realization Cadence
### Project Lighthouse — Knowledge Infrastructure Transformation

**Version:** 1.0
**Related:** `Benefits Register.xlsx`, `Transformation KPIs.md`

---

## 1. Why an Ongoing Cadence, Not a One-Time Business Case Check

Most transformation business cases are validated once, at approval, and rarely revisited with the same rigor after go-live. This transformation treats value realization as a **standing function** with a defined review cadence, owned beyond the life of the delivery project.

## 2. Review Cadence

| Review | Frequency | Owner | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery-Level KPI Tracking | Per sprint / migration wave | Program Manager | Tracks operational metrics (search success, grounding accuracy) during active delivery |
| Quarterly Benefits Review | Quarterly, starting Month 15 | Executive Sponsor | Reviews Benefits Register against targets; escalates if realization is lagging |
| Annual Value Audit | Annually, starting Year 1 post-launch | Transformation Steering Committee | Independent check of financial benefit realization against the original business case |

## 3. What Happens If Benefits Underperform

If a quarterly review shows a benefit tracking meaningfully below target (e.g., productivity gains below the estimated €1.2M/year run rate), the response follows an escalation similar to the delivery-level decision process: options are analyzed (adjust adoption strategy, revisit incentive design, extend the timeline for full realization) and a decision is documented, mirroring the discipline shown in the PM Delivery repository's Decisions folder.

## 4. Ownership Beyond Program Closure

Once the PM Delivery workstream formally closes, value realization ownership transfers to the Executive Sponsor and Knowledge Governance Council, per the Target Operating Model — ensuring benefits tracking doesn't quietly stop simply because the delivery project has ended.
