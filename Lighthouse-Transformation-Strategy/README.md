# Lighthouse Transformation Strategy
### Digital Transformation Lens — Project Lighthouse

*Part of the Project Lighthouse: Global Knowledge Infrastructure Program at Solstice Advisory. See the [Program Overview](../Project-Lighthouse) for the full business case and companion repository.*

---

## Executive Summary

This repository documents the **strategic and organizational transformation** required to introduce a global Knowledge Infrastructure platform at Solstice Advisory, a fictional global consulting firm. It covers the digital maturity assessment, target operating model, governance structure, technology strategy, and change management approach needed to shift how thousands of consultants across practices, industries, and geographies find and reuse organizational knowledge.

This repository focuses on **why and how the organization changes**. For the disciplined execution of the underlying delivery — schedule, budget, risk, stakeholder management — see the companion repository: [Lighthouse PM Delivery](../Lighthouse-PM-Delivery).

---

## Background

Solstice Advisory's knowledge is scattered across disconnected systems, inconsistently governed, and difficult to reuse across regional and practice boundaries — while also carrying constraints unique to professional services: client-confidentiality walls, contractual IP restrictions, and cross-border data regulations. Solving this is not simply a technology rollout; it requires redesigning how the firm governs, classifies, and incentivizes the use of its own knowledge globally. That is the scope of this repository.

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- **Strategic thinking** — connecting a technology initiative to firm-wide ambition and maturity
- **Organizational design** — building a Target Operating Model: roles, decision rights, governance bodies
- **Portfolio management** — sequencing interdependent initiatives, prioritization logic, dependency mapping
- **Governance design** — steering structures, escalation paths, federated vs. central decision authority
- **Value and benefits management** — treating value realization as an ongoing function, not a one-time calculation
- **Technology strategy** — platform philosophy, build-vs-buy stance, AI capability positioning (non-technical)
- **Change leadership at scale** — managing adoption fatigue and incentive misalignment across a global rollout
- **Domain fluency in professional services** — ethical walls, IP reuse, federated global/regional governance
