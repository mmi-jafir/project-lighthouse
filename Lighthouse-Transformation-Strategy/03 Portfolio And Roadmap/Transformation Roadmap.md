## 2. Workstream Summary

| Workstream | Approach | Duration | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governance & Taxonomy Design | Stage-gated | Months 1–3 | Knowledge Governance Lead |
| Migration | Hybrid (wave-based) | Months 4–13 | Migration Lead |
| Technology / Platform | Agile | Months 4–17 | Technology Lead |
| AI / RAG | Agile / Lean | Months 6–15 | Technology Lead |
| Change & Adoption | Iterative, continuous | Months 1–18 | Change Lead |
| Regional Rollout | Stage-gated (per region) | Months 14–17 | Program Manager |
| Value Realization | Continuous, post-launch | Month 17 onward | Executive Sponsor |

## 3. Portfolio Prioritization Logic

Workstreams were sequenced and prioritized according to the criteria detailed in `Portfolio Prioritization.xlsx` — weighing business value, dependency position, risk, and organizational readiness. Governance was prioritized first specifically because the Digital Maturity Assessment identified it as the binding constraint on every downstream workstream.
