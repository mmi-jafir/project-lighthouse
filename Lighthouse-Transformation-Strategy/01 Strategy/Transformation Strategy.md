# Transformation Strategy
### Project Lighthouse — Knowledge Infrastructure Transformation

**Version:** 1.0
**Related:** `Digital Maturity Assessment.md`, `Business Case.md`, `../02 Operating Model/Target Operating Model.md`

---

## 1. Vision

Transform Solstice Advisory's fragmented organizational knowledge into a governed, discoverable, reusable, and AI-ready enterprise knowledge ecosystem — enabling every consultant, in every region, to find and reuse the firm's collective expertise as fast as the best-connected office already does informally.

## 2. Strategic Rationale

The Digital Maturity Assessment identified governance and information architecture, not technology, as the firm's primary knowledge gap. A platform alone would not resolve this — the transformation must address **people, process, and governance alongside technology**, sequenced deliberately rather than deployed all at once.

## 3. Strategic Pillars

1. **Governance First** — establish ownership, classification standards, and a federated global/regional model before scaling technology
2. **Technology as Enabler** — deploy a unified platform with search and AI-assisted retrieval, built on governed content rather than raw migration
3. **Incentive-Aligned Adoption** — address the billable-hour disincentive directly, rather than relying on training and communication alone
4. **Confidentiality by Design** — ensure the firm's client-confidentiality and ethical-wall obligations are structural requirements of the platform, not bolted-on controls
5. **Value Realization as an Ongoing Function** — treat benefits tracking as a continuing capability, not a one-time business case exercise

## 4. Sequencing Logic

```text
Governance & Taxonomy Design
        ↓
Platform & Migration (regional waves)
        ↓
AI-Assisted Retrieval (gated by pilot performance)
        ↓
Adoption & Incentive Alignment (parallel throughout)
        ↓
Value Realization (continuous, post-launch)
```

This sequencing directly reflects the maturity assessment's finding that governance must precede technology, and the delivery-level lesson (see the PM Delivery repository) that a strong pilot gate protects the credibility of AI features.

## 5. Relationship to the PM Delivery Workstream

This transformation strategy is realized through disciplined project delivery, documented in full in the companion repository: **Lighthouse PM Delivery**. That repository shows how the strategic sequencing above translated into an actual 18-month schedule, budget, and set of delivery decisions.

## 6. Success Definition

The transformation succeeds if, 12 months post-launch, consultants across all three regions report the platform as their default starting point for finding organizational knowledge — not merely an additional tool alongside SharePoint and email.
