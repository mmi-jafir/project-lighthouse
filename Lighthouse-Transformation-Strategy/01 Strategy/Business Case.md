# Business Case
### Project Lighthouse — Knowledge Infrastructure Transformation

**Version:** 1.0
**Related:** `Digital Maturity Assessment.md`, `../06 Value Realization/Benefits Register.xlsx`

---

## 1. The Problem

Solstice Advisory's consultants spend significant time daily searching for organizational knowledge scattered across SharePoint, shared drives, and email, with no consistent classification and no unified search. Proposals are frequently rebuilt from scratch rather than reusing prior credentials and case work. Expertise is difficult to locate across a global, matrixed firm, and tacit knowledge is regularly lost to staff attrition.

## 2. Quantified Impact of Inaction

An estimated 15 minutes/day per consultant lost to searching, across the firm's global consultant base, represents a substantial recurring productivity cost — estimated at approximately €25,000/day firm-wide at current scale.

## 3. Proposed Investment

A €750,000 investment over 18 months to deliver a governed Knowledge Infrastructure platform, including migration, taxonomy and governance design, unified search, an AI-assisted retrieval layer, and adoption support.

## 4. Estimated Return

A 20% improvement in search efficiency is estimated to translate to approximately **€1.2M/year** in recovered productivity — a payback period of well under one year against the €750,000 investment — alongside less quantifiable but material benefits:

- Faster proposal turnaround through reuse of prior credentials and case assets
- Reduced knowledge loss from analyst/associate attrition
- Improved consistency of client-facing work through better access to firm methodology

## 5. Cost of Delay

Each year the transformation is delayed represents an estimated €1.2M in foregone productivity benefit, plus continued knowledge loss from ongoing staff turnover and continued risk exposure from inconsistent confidentiality controls across ungoverned content stores.

## 6. Risk-Adjusted View

The business case assumes a 20% search efficiency improvement, deliberately conservative relative to the up-to-85% search success rate targeted at the delivery level (see PM Delivery repository), to avoid overstating the benefit case to the Steering Committee.

*(All figures are simulated/estimated for portfolio purposes.)*
