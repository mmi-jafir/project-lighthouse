# Governance and Steering Structure
### Project Lighthouse — Knowledge Infrastructure Transformation

**Version:** 1.0
**Related:** `Target Operating Model.md`

---

## 1. Governance Bodies

| Body | Purpose | Membership | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transformation Steering Committee | Strategic oversight, gate approvals, major risk and budget decisions | Executive Sponsor, Regional Managing Partners, Program Manager | Monthly |
| Knowledge Governance Council | Global taxonomy and policy standards; resolves cross-regional conflicts | Global Knowledge Governance Lead, Regional Governance Contacts, Compliance representative | Bi-monthly, post-launch |
| Program Delivery Team | Day-to-day execution of the delivery workstream | Program Manager, Technology Lead, Migration Lead, Change Lead | Weekly |

## 2. Decision Escalation Path

```text
Working-level issue
      ↓
Program Delivery Team (resolve directly if within authority)
      ↓
Knowledge Governance Council (if a governance/policy question)
      ↓
Transformation Steering Committee (if budget, scope, or schedule impact)
      ↓
Executive Sponsor (if strategic or firm-wide implication)
```

This mirrors, and formalizes, the escalation path used operationally during delivery (see `Lighthouse-PM-Delivery/02 Planning/Communication Plan.md`).

## 3. Gate Reviews

| Gate | Purpose | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| Governance & Taxonomy Design Sign-off | Confirms federated model and taxonomy spine before migration begins | Knowledge Governance Council |
| Migration Wave Gate (per region) | Confirms classification quality before wave close | Transformation Steering Committee |
| AI Pilot Gate | Confirms AI assistant meets performance thresholds before release | Transformation Steering Committee |
| Program Closure | Confirms benefits realization and formal handover to operating model | Executive Sponsor |

## 4. Post-Launch Governance Transition

Once the PM Delivery workstream closes, governance responsibility transfers from the Program Delivery Team to the Knowledge Governance Council and Regional Contacts, per the Target Operating Model. The Transformation Steering Committee's role narrows to periodic value-realization review rather than active program management.
