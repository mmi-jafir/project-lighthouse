# Target Operating Model
### Project Lighthouse — Knowledge Infrastructure Transformation

**Version:** 1.0
**Related:** `Governance And Steering Structure.md`, `Federated vs Central Governance.md`

---

## 1. Purpose

Defines the roles, decision rights, and organizational structure required to run the Knowledge Infrastructure platform sustainably after the delivery project closes — the "who does what" that keeps the platform governed rather than reverting to fragmentation.

## 2. Operating Model Structure

```text
GLOBAL LAYER
│
├── Executive Sponsor (Global Managing Partner)
├── Knowledge Governance Council (global standards, cross-region escalation)
└── Global Knowledge Governance Lead (spine taxonomy, platform standards)

REGIONAL LAYER
│
├── Regional Knowledge Governance Contacts (EMEA, Americas, APAC)
├── Regional Extension Taxonomy Ownership
└── Regional Adoption Champions

PRACTICE LAYER
│
├── Practice Area Knowledge Stewards
└── Subject Matter Experts (SMEs)

FUNCTIONAL SUPPORT
│
├── Platform Engineering / Technology Team
├── Compliance & Data Privacy Office
└── HR / People Team (incentive and career-model alignment)
```

## 3. Role Definitions

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Executive Sponsor | Firm-wide accountability for the platform's ongoing value and funding |
| Knowledge Governance Council | Sets global standards; resolves cross-regional taxonomy or policy conflicts |
| Global Knowledge Governance Lead | Owns the taxonomy spine, content lifecycle policy, and platform governance standards |
| Regional Knowledge Governance Contacts | Own regional taxonomy extensions; coordinate local content quality and adoption |
| Practice Area Knowledge Stewards | Ensure practice-specific content accuracy and currency |
| Platform Engineering | Owns technical operation, search, and AI/RAG infrastructure |
| Compliance & Data Privacy Office | Owns confidentiality, ethical-wall, and data residency compliance |

## 4. Decision Rights (RACI Summary)

| Decision | Global Governance Council | Regional Contact | Practice Steward | Compliance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Global taxonomy spine changes | A/R | C | C | I |
| Regional extension tags | I | A/R | C | I |
| Content lifecycle policy | A/R | C | I | C |
| Confidentiality / access rules | C | I | I | A/R |
| Platform technical roadmap | A | C | I | C |

*(A = Accountable, R = Responsible, C = Consulted, I = Informed)*

## 5. Relationship to Delivery

This target operating model is the **end state** the PM Delivery workstream builds toward — the Knowledge Governance Lead and Regional Contacts referenced throughout the PM Delivery repository's Stakeholder Register and Decisions are the same roles formalized here.
